About CareerBridge AI

Career guidance built around your next step.

CareerBridge brings opportunity discovery, practical skill planning, and career preparation into one student-focused workspace.

Our approach

We help students connect their current skills and goals to real job and internship requirements. Recommendations are explainable: the application shows skill overlap alongside text similarity rather than presenting an unexplained score.

Resume feedback and interview guidance use clear, deterministic checks. They are intended as a helpful starting point—not a replacement for a recruiter, mentor, or professional career adviser.

How matching works

  1. Skill overlapCompares profile skills to skills listed on an opportunity.
  2. Text similarityUses scikit-learn TF-IDF and cosine similarity on profile and role text.
  3. Transparent resultCombines the two signals and lists matched and missing skills.
